## 2. Portal Overview & Value Proposition
The Production Planner is the conductor of the automotive stamping plant. In a press shop operating mechanical and hydraulic presses ranging from 20 to 250 tons, efficiency depends on minimizing die changeover downtime, optimizing strip nesting, balancing press load across shifts, and ensuring materials arrive before the press stops.

The Production Planner Portal provides the mathematical engine of the plant: exploding Bills of Materials (BOM) through automated MRP, identifying raw material coil shortages weeks in advance, verifying machine tonnage and operator capacity, generating sequenced Work Orders, and allocating stock to shopfloor jobs.

## 3. Complete Navigation & Detailed Menu Breakdown

### A. Material Planning / MRP Engine (`/mrp`)

#### 1. Run Material Plan (`/mrp/execution`)
- **Purpose:** The core calculation engine that computes gross and net requirements across all finished assemblies, stamped child parts, and raw materials.
- **Execution Parameters:**
  - *Planning Horizon:* Select date range (e.g. Next 30 Days, 60 Days, Quarter).
  - *Plant Selection:* Select target manufacturing plant.
  - *Demand Source:* Ingests confirmed Sales Orders + Customer Forecasted Demand.
- **MRP Calculation Logic:**
  - `Gross Requirement` = Confirmed Orders + Forecast Demand.
  - `Net Requirement` = Gross Requirement - (Stock on Hand + Open POs in Transit - Safety Stock Buffer).
  - Explodes multi-level BOM: Stamped sub-assembly ➔ individual brackets/clips ➔ sheet metal blank ➔ raw steel coil tonnage.
- **Actions:**
  - `Execute MRP`: Triggers background computation and updates shortage logs in seconds.

#### 2. Material Shortages (`/mrp/shortages`)
- **Purpose:** Early warning radar highlighting parts that will run short and on what specific date.
- **Information Displayed:**
  - Material Code & Specification (e.g. `RM-STL-CRCA-2.0MM`).
  - Required Quantity vs Free Available Quantity.
  - Projected Stockout Date (e.g. *Shortage of 4.2 Tons expected on 15-Oct*).
  - Linked Sales Orders / Work Orders at risk of delay.

#### 3. Suggested Orders (`/mrp/recommendations`)
- **Purpose:** Actionable buy and make orders generated by the MRP calculation.
- **Recommendation Categories:**
  - *Make Suggestions:* Generate internal Work Orders for stamping presses and welding cells.
  - *Buy Suggestions:* Generate Purchase Requisitions (PR) for raw coils and bought-out hardware with Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) rounding.
- **Actions:**
  - `Approve & Release`: Converts suggestions into official Work Orders and Purchase Requisitions with a single click.

### B. Capacity Planning Module (`/capacity`)

#### 1. Machine Capacity (`/capacity/machine`)
- **Purpose:** Compare press machine capacity against production demand to prevent line overload and bottlenecks.
- **Metrics & Visuals:**
  - Machine groups: 20T Presses, 50T Presses, 100T Presses, 250T Heavy Hydraulic Presses, Projection Welders.
  - Available Machine Hours (Operating hours × shifts × working days).
  - Required Hours (Planned parts × operation cycle time / SPM).
  - Capacity Utilization Bar: Color-coded green (<85%), yellow (85-95%), red (>95% bottleneck alert).

#### 2. Manpower Capacity (`/capacity/labor`)
- **Purpose:** Verify press operator and welder staffing requirements across shifts.
- **Functionality:**
  - Calculates required operator-hours based on routing labor standards.
  - Highlights staffing deficits or surplus across Shift 1 (General), Shift 2, and Shift 3 (Night).

#### 3. Production Schedule (`/capacity/schedule`)
- **Purpose:** Day-by-day sequence of jobs per press work centre.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Visual Gantt timeline displaying planned job cards.
  - Sequences jobs by die family and coil thickness to minimize die changeover downtime (SMED principles).

---

### C. Production Management (`/production`)

#### 1. Production Plan (`/production/plan`)
- **Purpose:** Master Production Schedule (MPS) defining monthly and weekly manufacturing targets.
- **Capabilities:**
  - Lock monthly production bucket targets per vehicle model line.
  - Track planned vs actual achievement percentages in real time.

#### 2. Work Orders (`/production/wo`)
- **Purpose:** Official production job cards released to the shop floor.
- **Key Fields & Data:**
  - Work Order ID (`WO-2026-XXXX`).
  - Product to manufacture (Part Number, Description, Drawing Rev).
  - Planned Quantity, Scrap Allowance %, Start Date, Due Date.
  - Assigned Press Work Centre and Press Tool / Die Code (e.g. `DIE-BRK-04`).
  - Routing steps: Blanking ➔ Stamping ➔ Piercing ➔ Deburring ➔ Sub-Assembly.
- **Actions:**
  - `Create Work Order`: Manual creation or release from MRP suggestion.
  - `Release to Floor`: Transitions status from `Draft` to `Released`, enabling material issuance and operator job start.
  - `Print Job Card / Routing Sheet`: Prints physical job card with barcode, BOM list, and inspection checkpoints for the press operator.

#### 3. Allocate Material (`/production/allocation`)
- **Purpose:** Hard-reserving specific raw material inventory to released work orders.
- **Functionality:**
  - Select Work Order ➔ System displays required steel coil specifications.
  - Planner selects specific coil Heat Numbers from warehouse stock and locks them against the job card.
  - Prevents other work orders or lines from accidentally consuming the designated steel coil.

---

### D. Engineering Visibility (Read-Only)

#### 1. Parts Lists / BOM (`/engineering/bom`)
- View multi-level engineering BOM: Finished part ➔ Stamped components ➔ Raw material blank dimensions, strip width, pitch, and scrap skeleton %.

#### 2. Process Steps / Routing (`/engineering/routing`)
- View standard operation sequences, machine tonnage requirements, tool setup times, and standard strokes per minute (SPM).

#### 3. Design Change Requests (`/engineering/change`)
- Track customer engineering change orders (ECO) to ensure work orders are released only against the latest drawing revision.

## 4. Live Client Demonstration Script (10-Minute Walkthrough)

1. **Log in as `production.planner@lbap.in`**:
   - Introduce this portal as the operational brain of the factory where demand is translated into shopfloor instructions.
2. **Open Run Material Plan (`/mrp/execution`)**:
   - Click `Execute MRP` and show how the system immediately evaluates customer orders, stock on hand, and lead times.
3. **Open Material Shortages (`/mrp/shortages`)**:
   - Point out the exact date a raw material will run short: *"The system alerts PPC before the shop floor ever runs out of steel."*
4. **Open Machine Capacity (`/capacity/machine`)**:
   - Show the press tonnage utilization charts. Demonstrate how planners identify bottleneck presses (e.g. 150T press at 108% load) and re-route jobs to alternative presses.
5. **Open Work Orders (`/production/wo`)**:
   - Create and release a Work Order. Show how it links the part number, routing steps, press tool/die code, and planned quantity.
6. **Open Allocate Material (`/production/allocation`)**:
   - Lock a specific coil heat number to the work order, demonstrating how Precision ERP prevents material mix-ups on the floor.
